This artifact presents a finite labelled model with fixed goal-reachable abstractions, a fixed order, and finite nonnegative label costs. It gives the saturation invariant, path-relay proof, tight support-two Fibonacci bound and matching construction, exact table-reuse criterion, ordered-repair proof, and counterexamples. Exact integer and rational checks cover implementation behavior and concrete witnesses; the evaluation protocol documents the bounded synthetic measurements.
